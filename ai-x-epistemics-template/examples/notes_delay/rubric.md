# Notes delay rubric

`sample_notes.csv` is synthetic. The first line says SYNTHETIC. Do not describe its median or its late share as a Community Notes result.

Delay is `note_at` minus `post_at`, in hours. The metric `median_hours` is the middle delay. `share_after_24h` is the share of notes strictly more than 24 hours after the post.

To study the real system, replace this file with the public Community Notes download and say so in the report. Keep the SYNTHETIC marker off that file only when the rows are actually from that download.
