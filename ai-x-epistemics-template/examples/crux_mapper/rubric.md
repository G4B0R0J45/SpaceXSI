# Crux mapper rubric

Transcript lines that match `CLAIM: <text> | P: <probability>` are statements. Other lines are ignored.

- The same person stating one claim at probabilities that differ by at least 0.25 is an inconsistency.
- Both people stating one claim with a gap of at least 0.25 is a crux. The comparison uses each person's latest probability.
- A smaller gap is shared ground.
- The interviews in this folder are fictional. Do not replace them with real participant text unless the dual-use section explains consent and retention.

`map.html` is regenerated by `python -m epistemics run crux_mapper`. Open that file. There is no server.
