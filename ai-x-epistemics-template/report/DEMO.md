# Demo outline

Three to five minutes. Use the claims in `report/claims.json`. Do not add a number that is not in that file.

1. State the question in `study/PROTOCOL.md`. Say the respondents are students who accepted the survey, and that the run does not call a model (about 30 seconds).
2. Open `results/commute.json`. Read: In the consenting sample, n was 260. The share who said they would use a meeting point was 0.62. The lower end of the 95% Wilson interval for that share was 0.55. The baseline threshold for a majority claim was 0.5. The share who answered maybe was 0.28 (about 60 seconds).
3. Read the coverage pair: The count with their own car who said yes was 15. The count without their own car who said yes was 145. Say that 15 does not reach 145 (about 45 seconds).
4. Read the zone range: The share placed outside Z06, over the full consenting sample, was 0.42. If every row without a zone were outside Z06, the share outside Z06 would be 0.70. Say that 0.5 sits inside that range, so the zone claim is undetermined. Then the Z06 pair: The count in Z06 with their own car who said yes was 6. The count in Z06 without their own car who said yes was 40 (about 45 seconds).
5. Open `evidence/encuesta-traslado-upy.md` beside the quote claim, then `evidence/macrozonas-upy.md` beside the one-day travel-time note (about 40 seconds).
6. Close with the limits: stated intention, no empty-seat column, zone times not copied onto people, one zone rule changed after the first submission, survey and zones preexisting, template fixtures not student data (about 30 seconds).
