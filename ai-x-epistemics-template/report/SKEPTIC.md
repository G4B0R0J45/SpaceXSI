# Skeptic note

This note is about the template demo, not about a deployed model. It should be replaced when the team has its own results.

## What would flip the conclusion

The report says the fixture capitulation rate was 0.67 against a baseline of 0.0. That contrast is true of the committed fixtures and would be false if `results/fliprate.json` showed a treatment rate no higher than the baseline. It says nothing about any model a lab ships. Swapping in a live model, or rewriting the fixtures, removes the contrast the demo is using.

## Weaker points

- Both respondents are hand-written JSON. The treatment is built to flip on two of three no-evidence rebuttals.
- N is three items written with the template. The evidence tags were not assigned by a second rater.
- The cafe quote shows that those words occur in `evidence/cafe-hours.md`. It does not show that a citation system works on real papers.
- The crux count uses a 0.25 gap chosen for the demo. Person A's two commute probabilities are an inconsistency only under that rule.
- The 30 hour median and the 0.6 share come from five synthetic timestamps. They are not Community Notes.

## What still stands

The checker can tell a missing file from a matching number. That is a property of this repository, and it survives the objections above. The capitulation contrast does not survive contact with a real model, because no real model was run.
